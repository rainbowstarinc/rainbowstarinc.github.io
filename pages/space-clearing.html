For Your Space

Space & Land Clearing

Energetic harmonization for homes, land, and sacred spaces.

The work

Clearing & harmonizing

Spaces hold energy. Old arguments, grief, transitions, the accumulation of generations — all of it lives in the walls, the land, the corners of a room. When a space feels stuck, heavy, or off, that's the field speaking.

Space and land clearing is the practice of listening to that field — and then working with it. Releasing what has accumulated, harmonizing what remains, and welcoming in what is meant to be there.

I work in person for local spaces and by distance for homes anywhere. Both are effective.

When to call

Good moments

· Moving into a new home or space

· After a difficult period, loss, or conflict

· Before a new chapter, birth, or beginning

· When a space feels heavy, stagnant, or off

· Land that carries old history or trauma

· Seasonal or ceremonial blessing of a space
