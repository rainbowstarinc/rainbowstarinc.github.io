Kids, Babies & Animals

Gentle sessions for the little ones and creatures — in person or by distance.

Tender beings

Working with the little ones

Children, babies, and animals are often the most receptive recipients of energy work. They haven't learned yet to filter or doubt — they simply receive. Sessions are adapted to honor that sensitivity.

With young children, sessions are often shorter, lighter, and playful. With babies, the work is very gentle and quiet. With animals, I follow their lead completely — they choose how close, and for how long.

Distance sessions work beautifully for animals and little ones who might be too wriggly or overwhelmed for an in-person visit. The energy arrives just the same.

· Animals experiencing fear, grief, or illness

· Pets facing end of life

· Families navigating big change together

· Parents who want support alongside their child
