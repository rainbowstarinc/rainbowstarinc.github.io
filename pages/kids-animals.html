Animal Reiki

The Wordless World

My path into animal work was not a straight line. I witnessed harm to animals as a young child, and it left me blocked — unable to access what I later understood was a natural channel of communication.

Twenty years of working with children and their animals — through nannying, teaching piano, dance, and drama — slowly moved those blocks. The animals came with the kids. I watched, I listened. Something opened.

The naturalist and author Martha Beck writes about communicating with animals beyond words — beyond ordinary thought. Reading her work, I recognized the capacity I had been quietly building. I trust it now.

I've always been drawn to rescue — to giving a chance to the creature that might otherwise be passed by. Animal rescue, habitat restoration, and the deep therapeutic nature of the human-animal bond are all part of why this work matters to me.

The body knows things the mind can't explain.

— Martha Beck

Distance Sessions

How It Works

Animals don't need to be on a video call to receive Reiki. Energy is not bound by location — and animals, unburdened by the need to understand how it works, often receive it with particular ease.

ONE

We Talk

A short video call with you first — what's happening with your animal, what's happening around them, what they love.

TWO

They Rest

Your animal settles in their favorite spot. They don't need to do anything. Animals receive beautifully — they frequently fall deeply asleep.

THREE

I Send

I work with their energy field from wherever I am. The distance changes nothing about the potency of the work.

FOUR

You Receive

A written or voice message with impressions and anything that came through — plus an optional recording of the session music.

For the Human

The Owner Component

Animals don't exist in isolation from their people. The bond is a living field — and it moves in both directions.

Animals mirror their owners' nervous systems. A stressed owner often has an anxious pet — and until something shifts in the field between them, the animal can't fully settle.

Owners sometimes unknowingly reinforce the very behavior they're trying to change — through their own fear or frustration in the moment.

And sometimes the animal is trying to help — carrying something for the human, protecting them — in a way that creates its own strain. Part of the work is helping the bond itself become a source of ease.

Kids & Babies

Children and babies are often the most receptive recipients of energy work. They haven't learned yet to filter or doubt — they simply receive.

With young children, sessions are often shorter, lighter, and playful. With babies, the work is very gentle and quiet. I follow their lead completely.

Distance sessions work beautifully for little ones who might be too wriggly or overwhelmed for an in-person visit.
