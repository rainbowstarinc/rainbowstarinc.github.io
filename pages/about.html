True Story

Here I Am

My name is Kezia Zamora — and RainbowStar emerged from years of walking the intersection of creative expression and the healing arts, discovering that the body, the brush, and the cosmos are all speaking the same language.

My work is rooted in a deep reverence for the body's intelligence, the wisdom of plant medicines, and the organizing field of love that underlies all things.

Sacred sensuality is part of my presence and my practice — I hold the understanding that eros and spirit are not separate, and that the body's pleasure is part of the healing path.

Each painting I make is a transmission. Each session I hold is a collaboration with something larger.

I show up as a conduit of source energy, your healing belongs to you.
