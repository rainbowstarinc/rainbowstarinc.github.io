What Cannabliss is

Cannabliss is a group exploration — intimate, intentional, and self-led. You bring your own cannabis and your own intention. I bring the container: held space, energetic support, and steady presence as you journey inward.

There is no guide in the traditional sense. The plant is your guide. I am here to hold the field — to witness, to tend, to weave Reiki through the gathering as you go wherever you're called to go.

This is for those who feel ready to explore cannabis as a spiritual tool: in community, with energy work woven throughout. Curiosity alone is welcome here — readiness grows in the doing.
