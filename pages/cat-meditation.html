The Story

22 Cats & a Meditation Space

I am surrounded by 22 cats in Greece who have strong opinions about everything — including meditation.

Every week I host a gentle online gathering: a guided meditation, a card reading, some grounding, some laughter, and whatever the cats decide to contribute.

This is not a serious space. It is a sacred one. The difference matters.
