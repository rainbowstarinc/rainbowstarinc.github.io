# Homebody — Event Planning Doc
*last updated: May 2026 · living document*

---

## Concept in Brief
A nervous-system friendly, recurring movement gathering in Portland, OR.
Live improvisational music. Movers who play, musicians who move.
Grounded, earthy, a little dorky. No steps to learn. No cool points required.

Influences: 5 Rhythms, Ecstatic Dance, Contact Improv, Mindful Movement, Somatic Practices

**Tagline:** *come home to your body*
**URL:** rainbowstar.net/homebody
**Day:** Thursday evenings
**Season:** Summer 2026 to start

---

## Format
- One-off first, then recurring series if it lands
- Indoor and outdoor mix
- Small, intimate — connective, you can know the whole room
- Consistent container/structure with rotating themes

### Session Arc (each gathering)
1. **Arrival** — doors open early, ambient sound, no pressure to move
2. **Opening circle** — brief, names, theme for the evening, agreements
3. **The Wave** — slow build → fuller expression → peak → dissolving → stillness
4. **Integration** — contact improv optional, musicians may put down instruments and move
5. **Closing circle** — one word out
6. **After-glow** — music continues, people linger

## Ticketing — Decision Pending

Options to consider:

| Platform | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Humanitix** | Ethical/charitable model, sliding scale friendly, aligns with values | Less familiar to attendees |
| **Eventbrite** | Most recognizable, easy for attendees, sliding scale possible | Higher fees, corporate |
| **Brown Paper Tickets** | Indie-event friendly | Clunkier UX |
| **Tito** | Clean, simple, free for free events | Less known |
| **Google Form + Venmo/PayPal** | No fees, personal, great for small intimate events | Manual tracking, less professional |

**Recommendation to consider:** For a first intimate gathering, a **Google Form + sliding scale Venmo** keeps it personal and zero-fee. Move to Humanitix when the series is established — it fits the values well.
