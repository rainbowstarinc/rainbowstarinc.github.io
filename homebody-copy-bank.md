## FACEBOOK EVENT — Series Description

*For a Facebook Event covering the full Homebody series. Pin this to the top.*

---

**Homebody** is a recurring movement gathering for bodies in motion — live acoustic music, free movement, small and intimate.

The music is live and unamplified (or close to it). No DJ, no PA, no walls of sound — just instruments and voices in the room with you. Musicians play freeform and responsively, following the energy of the group. They're also invited to move. Movers are invited to make sound.

Each Thursday evening follows a simple arc: arrival, opening circle, a wave of movement that builds and dissolves, integration, and a closing circle. There may be a brief teaching or invitation at the start — something to listen with, explore with.

**This is for you if:**
You love music and movement but don't want a performance space. You're curious about ecstatic dance, 5 Rhythms, or contact improv but want something small first. Your nervous system appreciates a gentle on-ramp. You like wholesome. You don't mind a little dorky.

**Practical things:**
📅 Thursday evenings · beginning May 21, 2026
📍 Indoor & outdoor locations in Portland, OR (location announced per gathering)
💛 Sliding scale $15–30 · scholarship tickets available
🌿 Alcohol-free · plant medicines welcome with care and intention
✋ Consent culture · go at your own pace · all bodies welcome · floor work welcome
